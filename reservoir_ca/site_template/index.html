About these estimates

Each reservoir's water surface area is measured from Sentinel-1 radar (which sees through cloud) and Sentinel-2 optical imagery, both processed in Google Earth Engine. Area is converted to volume with a curve fit for each reservoir by pairing past area measurements with the storage the California Data Exchange Center (CDEC) reported on the same day. Radar and optical estimates of the same reservoir can differ by several percent, so the totals move a little from day to day as new images arrive.
